# Skin Treatment Banner Templates

Editable canvas: https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c5e85b44-2e4d-430c-b13b-754ff6fa60e4

Three roll-up banner designs (1:2 proportion, authored at 800 x 1600 px) rebuilt from the
original "banner template.pdf" in the Drive **Banners** folder, with every United Academy
reference removed. Each banner carries dashed "YOUR LOGO" slots. The photos ship in the template and are ready
to use as they are, or a student can swap in their own: the hero, before/after and concern
photos are cropped from the original PDF (old logo removed), and the treatment photo is
Dr. Rusnak's own ISR demonstration photo from Drive.

| Artboard | File | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treatment | `Main.dc.html` | Faded hero photo + logo, "Scar and stretch mark treatment" headline, before/after photos, tagline |
| Concerns | `Concerns.dc.html` | "Targeted solution for skin imperfections", three photos, list of concerns, full-bleed treatment photo with logo |
| Co-Brand | `CoBrand.dc.html` | Dark banner with the student's logo, a plus sign, and a partner logo slot |

The `.jpg` files are the embedded photos. `canvas.json` lays the three out side by side and holds the how-to-use note shown on the canvas.

Fonts: Montserrat (headlines and body) and Cormorant Garamond (logo placeholder text), both from
Google Fonts. Tweaks above each artboard: one accent color on the two white banners, background
and accent color on the dark one.
